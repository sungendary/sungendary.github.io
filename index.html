About

I am an undergraduate student majoring in Artificial Intelligence at Kyonggi University. I previously worked as an undergraduate research assistant at the AI Vision Lab under the guidance of Prof. Junho Ahn, where I conducted research on 3D LiDAR-based perception for autonomous driving. Before that, I was a member of the RO:AD club, where I gained hands-on experience with autonomous driving platforms such as ROS2, Autoware, and F1TENTH.

My interest lies in building reliable autonomous driving systems. Rather than focusing on a single module, I am interested in how perception, planning, and validation work together as a whole system, and in how rule-based and AI-based approaches can be combined to achieve both high performance and trustworthiness. My work addresses this goal through three main directions:

  • Perception: I aim to help vehicles understand their surroundings accurately by detecting and tracking 3D objects, fusing information from multiple sensors, localizing the vehicle within a map, and identifying lanes and drivable areas.
  • Decision Making & Motion Planning: I am interested in how a vehicle decides what to do next and how it should move, from high-level behavior planning to generating safe trajectories and controlling the vehicle to follow them.
  • Validation: I study how to verify that autonomous driving systems behave as intended, by evaluating their performance in both simulation and real vehicles and by testing them across diverse driving scenarios.

If you have any questions or would like to chat, please feel free to reach out anytime.
